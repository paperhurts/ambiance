## Privacy on GitHub (standing rule)
- Nothing pushed to or posted on GitHub (code, comments, commit messages, PR titles and descriptions,
  review replies) may carry personal information: no names of the owner or their family, no family
  or home details, no personal photo or video links. Refer to the owner neutrally, or not at all.
- No Claude session links anywhere on GitHub: leave the `Claude-Session:` trailer out of commit
  messages and the session URL out of PR descriptions and comments.
- The one exception: the "Made for …" dedication on the About screen stays. The owner chose to keep it.
